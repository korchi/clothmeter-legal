# ClothMeter Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 5 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the ClothMeter app ("the app", "we") processes, why, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. Who is responsible

## 2. Summary

| What | When | Why | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photos you measure, measurements, settings | Always, **only on your device** | To run the app | Until you delete the app or the photo |
| Problem reports (see 4.1) | Only when you tap **Send** | To fix detection problems you report | Up to 24 months |
| Usage data (see 4.2) | Only if you **allow** it | To improve detection and the app | Up to 13 months |
| Outlines and corrections (see 4.3) | Only if you **allow** it | To improve where measurement points are placed | Up to 24 months |
| Minimum-version check (see 4.4) | When the app opens | To keep the app working and secure | Not stored by us |

We do not sell data, show ads, track you across other apps or websites, or use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## 3. What stays on your device

- **Camera and photos.** Photos you take or pick, the garment outline and all measurements are processed on your device. They are not sent to us unless you include the photo in a problem report.
- **Saving to Photos.** The app adds annotated images to your photo library only when you tap Save, and only asks for permission to add photos, not to read your library.
- **Settings.** Your choices (units, decimal places, display options, your answers to the privacy questions) are stored on your device.

## 4. Data we receive

All data we receive is stored in the app's database on Apple's iCloud service (CloudKit, see section 5). CloudKit attaches an identifier that is specific to this app and your iCloud account to each record. From it we cannot see your Apple Account, name or email address.

### 4.1 Problem reports

When you fill in **Report a Problem** and tap **Send**, we receive:

- the problems you selected and your comment;
- the photo, only if **Include the photo** is on;
- the detected garment outline and measurement points, and your corrections;
- your email address, only if you enter one;
- if you allowed usage data, your recent steps in the app;
- your device model, system version and app version.

**Purpose:** to understand and fix the problem, and to use the report as a test case when improving automatic detection.
**Legal basis:** your consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), given by sending the report.
**Retention:** deleted at the latest 24 months after you sent it, or earlier when you delete it (section 6).

### 4.2 Usage data

Only if you tap **Allow** when asked, or turn on **Share Anonymous Usage Data** in Settings, the app records how it is used and sends it to us when the app goes to the background:

- which screens and tools you use, for example taking a photo, fixing the outline, cropping, saving or sharing;
- how far you moved measurement points away from where the app placed them, and whether detection succeeded;
- device model, system version and app version.

Usage data contains no photos, no measurement values and no contact details.

**Purpose:** to find where automatic detection and the app need improvement.
**Legal basis:** your consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), and for storing this information on your device, the national rules implementing Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive.
**Retention:** deleted at the latest 13 months after it was recorded.
**Withdrawing consent:** turn off **Share Anonymous Usage Data** in Settings. This stops recording and erases what has not been sent yet.

### 4.3 Outlines and corrections

Only if you tap **Allow** when asked, or turn on **Share Outlines and Corrections** in Settings, the app sends the following when you leave a measured photo in which you moved measurement points or fixed the outline. It also sends a random 20 % of measured photos in which you changed nothing, so we can see how often detection is right:

- the garment outline the app detected (a black-and-white silhouette, not the photo), and your corrected outline if you fixed it;
- where the app placed each measurement point, where you moved it, and when;
- the garment type, how the outline was chosen, whether you saved or shared the result;
- device model, system version and app version.

Outlines and corrections contain no photos, no measurement values and no contact details. An outline shows the shape of the garment.

**Purpose:** to measure and improve how accurately the app places measurement points.
**Legal basis:** your consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), and for storing this information on your device until it is sent, the national rules implementing Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive.
**Retention:** deleted at the latest 24 months after it was sent.
**Withdrawing consent:** turn off **Share Outlines and Corrections** in Settings. This stops sending and erases what has not been sent yet.

### 4.4 Minimum-version check

When the app opens, it reads the oldest supported app version from our iCloud database, so that outdated versions can ask you to update. This only reads data; nothing about you is stored by us. Apple processes technical connection data, such as your IP address, to deliver it.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and secure, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f).

## 5. Service providers and transfers

We use **Apple iCloud (CloudKit)** to store problem reports, usage data, outlines and corrections. Apple processes this data on our behalf. Apple may store it on servers outside the European Economic Area, including the United States. According to Apple, such transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. See Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

We do not share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** the data we hold about you and get a copy (Art. 15, 20);
- **correct** it (Art. 16);
- **delete** it (Art. 17): tap **Settings → Delete My Data** in the app. This deletes your problem reports, usage data, outlines and corrections from our iCloud database and everything the app recorded on your device. Copies we have already downloaded for analysis are also deleted when they reach the retention limits above; to have them deleted sooner, email us;
- **restrict** or **object** to processing (Art. 18, 21);
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing that happened before (Art. 7(3)).

To exercise these rights, email karel.durkota@gmail.com. Because reports are linked only to an anonymous iCloud identifier, we may ask for details from your report, such as the date and device, to find your data.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work. Our lead authority is the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), https://uoou.gov.cz.

## 7. Children

ClothMeter is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 8. Security

Data in transit and at rest in iCloud is encrypted by Apple. Access to reports and usage data is limited to accounts we explicitly authorize.

## 9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
