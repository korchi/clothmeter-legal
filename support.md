# ClothMeter Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **[karel.durkota@gmail.com](mailto:karel.durkota@gmail.com?subject=ClothMeter%20support)**. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version. Replies usually come within a few days.

If a measurement point or the garment outline is in the wrong place, the quickest way to tell us is **Report a Problem** in the app (the **…** menu on the photo). It sends the case along, so it can be fixed in the next version.

## Getting accurate measurements

- **Lay the garment flat** and smooth out wrinkles. Spread the sleeves slightly away from the body, and separate the legs of pants.
- **Use a plain surface** that contrasts with the garment: a light shirt on a dark floor, a dark shirt on a light table or bed.
- **Hold the phone straight above** the garment, parallel to the surface. The level indicator turns green when the phone is level.
- **Fit the whole garment** inside the outline on the screen, with a little space around it.
- **Good, even light** helps the camera find the surface. Avoid strong shadows across the garment.

## Questions

**Do I need a ruler, a tape measure or a reference card?**
No. The camera measures the table, bed or floor the garment lies on, and calculates real sizes from it.

**Which iPhones are supported?**
An iPhone with ARKit (iPhone XS or newer) running iOS 18 or later.

**A point is in the wrong place. What can I do?**
Drag it to the right spot; points snap to the garment's edge. You can turn snapping off under **View → Snap to Garment Edge**.

**The outline missed a sleeve, or picked up part of the floor.**
Tap **Fix Garment Outline**, then tap the part that's missing (or the part that doesn't belong). **Start over** lets you select the garment from scratch.

**Can I measure a photo I took earlier?**
Yes. Choose it from your photo library on the camera screen. Photos from the library have no camera measurements, so tap one measurement and enter its real size, for example the length you measured once with a tape; all other sizes are calculated from it.

**How do I switch between centimetres and inches?**
On the photo, open **View → Units**. The app remembers your choice.

**How do I get a clean picture for a listing?**
Use **View → Remove Background** and **View → Measurement Table**, then **Save** to your photo library or share it.

**A garment type I need is missing.**
Pick the closest type and add your own line with **… → Add Measurement**. Let us know which type you'd like to see.

**Does the app need an account?**
No. There's nothing to sign up for or sign in to.

## Privacy

Photos are processed on your iPhone. Problem reports are sent only when you tap Send; anonymous usage data and garment outlines only if you allow them. You can change these choices in **Settings**, and **Settings → Delete My Data** erases what you sent from our iCloud database.
